I’m a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon Prime Video where I’m part of the Playback Intelligence Team. I'm interested in leveraging Applied ML/AI and Optimization techniques for problems in Internet Video including Adaptive Streaming, Content Delivery and Performance Observability. I received my PhD from USC where I was advised by [Ramesh Govindan](https://govindan.usc.edu/).

I’m also an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the Computer Science Department at [NCSU](https://www.csc.ncsu.edu/) where I contribute to work on emergent Multimedia applications.
